Methodology

EcoScore India transforms raw CPCB Air Quality Index (AQI) data into a more intuitive EcoQI Score, ranging from 0 to 100, where higher is better.

The Problem with Raw AQI

Standard AQI uses a scale where lower is better (0-50 is Good). This can be unintuitive when combined with other livability scores where 100% is the ideal target. Furthermore, the bands are not linear.
We apply a piecewise mathematical transformation to invert the AQI scale into a 0-100 grade, which we call EcoQI. The formula ensures that severe pollution drops the score rapidly.

CPCB AQI Range Category EcoQI Score Range Letter Grade
0 - 50 Good 80.0 - 100.0 A+ or A
51 - 100 Satisfactory 50.0 - 79.9 B
101 - 200 Moderate 15.0 - 49.9 C
201 - 300 Poor 5.0 - 14.9 D
301 - 400+ Very Poor / Severe 0.0 - 4.9 F
